    Experience

    • Support students through in-class assistance, weekly studio hours and tutorials.
    • Demonstrate digital imaging and camera workflows and troubleshoot technical issues.
    • Foster an inclusive studio environment through peer mentorship, course logistics and respect for student confidentiality.
    • Designed and planned a campus Planet Walk, a scaled solar system route running from the Planetarium to the Observatory. Installation is still to come.
    • Developed a system of ceramic planetary markers, backed by 66 material tests; the production tiles are made and ready to glaze and fire. Designed the Observatory mural, printed on vinyl, with an on-site mockup.
    • Built an interactive digital companion with a campus map, planet pages and a walk tracker.
    • Lead the visual identity and social media presence, creating cohesive digital and print content for interfaith programming.
    • Designed and deployed “A Digital Moving Mountain,” an interactive web platform for the exhibition Footprints Across the Ocean featuring artist Dr. Gan Yu.
    • Engineered the site architecture to visualize complex artistic themes.
    • Authored a catalogue essay on the project’s technical methodology with faculty mentors.
    • Organized campus events on cultural exchange and inclusivity, leading teams of student staff.
    • Developed leadership skills working with diverse student groups to foster a sense of belonging.
